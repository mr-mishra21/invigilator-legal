## 2. What Invigilator Does

Invigilator is a study supervision application designed for students preparing for competitive examinations (such as JEE, NEET, APSC/UPSC and similar). The App monitors which applications a student opens during a self-declared "focus session" and provides voice reminders and notifications when the student switches to distracting applications such as social media or video apps.

The App is used in two primary modes:

- **Student mode:** The student uses the App on their own phone to track their focus sessions and receive reminders.
- **Parent mode:** A parent or legal guardian can link to a student's account to view summaries of the student's focus sessions, including counts of reminders and nag notifications received.

The App is intended for users aged 13 years and above, with **mandatory verifiable parental consent for users under 18**.

## 4. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the data necessary for Invigilator to function. The categories are:

### A. Account Information
- Phone number (used for authentication via Google Firebase Auth)
- Date of birth (used to determine whether parental consent is required)
- Display name (chosen by you)
- Role on the platform (student or parent)
- Selected app language (English, Hindi, Assamese, or Bengali)

### B. Linking Data (for parent-student relationships)
- Linking codes generated by parents and entered by students
- Records of which parent account is linked to which student account
- Consent records (consent type, version of consent text, signed name, timestamp, identifier of consenter, identifier of subject)

### C. Session Monitoring Data
- Start and end times of focus sessions you initiate
- Session type (Timed or Open-ended) and duration
- Package names of apps opened during your sessions (e.g., "com.google.android.youtube")
- Classification by the App of each opened app as "study", "neutral", or "distracting"
- Times at which voice reminders ("nudges") or nag notifications were issued
- Counts of nudges and nags per session

### D. App Usage Information

The App requires Android's "Usage Access" permission (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS) to detect which app is currently in the foreground during a focus session. We use this **only** to identify whether the foreground app is study-related or distracting.

**We do not collect:** screen content, screenshots, URLs visited, text typed, audio, video, or any content within the apps you use. We collect only the package name and only during active focus sessions you have started.

### E. Diagnostic Data (via Google Firebase Crashlytics)
When the App crashes or encounters an error, we collect:
- Device model and Android operating system version
- App version and build number
- Stack trace of the crash
- An anonymous installation identifier

This helps us identify and fix bugs. Crashlytics data is processed by Google LLC and stored on Google's servers, including locations outside India (primarily the United States).

### F. App Analytics Data (via Google Firebase Analytics)
We use Firebase Analytics to understand basic usage patterns of the App:
- Screen views (which screens within the App you visit)
- Number of times the App is opened
- Approximate region (country level only; no precise location)
- Device model and operating system version
- An anonymous user identifier (auto-generated by Firebase)

We do not link this anonymous identifier to your name or phone number, although Google may correlate Analytics data with other data Google holds about you. Firebase Analytics data is processed by Google and stored on Google's servers, primarily in the United States.

### G. What We Do NOT Collect
- Precise location (no GPS access)
- Contacts, photos, or files
- Screen content or screenshots
- URLs visited or web browsing data
- Audio recordings (no microphone access)
- Camera or video
- Financial or payment information
- Government identification numbers (Aadhaar, PAN, etc.)

- Your account and session data are stored in **Google Firebase Firestore**, hosted in Google's `asia-south1` data centre region (Mumbai, India).

*DPDP Act requires "reasonable security safeguards." We believe the above are the "reasonable security safeguards" under the DPDP Act.*

## 10. Children and Minors

Invigilator processes personal data of minors (under 18 in India) only with verifiable parental or guardian consent.

**How parental consent works:**
1. A parent creates an account and obtains a unique linking code via the App
2. The student enters this linking code in their App
3. Before the linking is finalised, the parent is presented with a consent notice describing what data will be collected from the minor and for what purposes
4. The parent records consent by signing their name and confirming
5. The consent record is stored with timestamp, parent's name, content hash of the consent text, parent's identifier, and the minor's identifier

**Restrictions for minor accounts:**
- We do not target minors with advertising of any kind
- We do not undertake behavioural tracking or profiling of minors beyond what is necessary for the App's stated purpose
- Parental consent can be withdrawn at any time by the parent deleting their account or the student's linked account

## 13. Permissions We Request and Why

The App requests the following permissions on your Android device:

- **Usage Access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS):** This special permission is required to detect which app is currently in the foreground during your focus session, so that we can identify when you have switched to a distracting app and issue voice reminders or notifications. Without this permission, the core function of the App cannot work. The permission is granted via Android Settings (the App cannot grant it automatically) and can be revoked at any time.
- **Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS):** Required on Android 13 and above to deliver focus reminder notifications during your sessions. You may disable this if you prefer voice-only reminders without notifications.
- **Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC):** Required by Android to keep the App active during a focus session so that monitoring and reminders work reliably.
- **Internet:** Required to connect to Firebase (authentication, data storage, crash reporting, analytics).

**We never request:** location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, files/storage, or other sensitive permissions.
